One login. Every internal tool.

Identra SSO drops into your existing directory and gives every internal web app a single, familiar login screen — no rip-and-replace, no new user database to maintain.

Directory-native

Identra authenticates straight against your existing directory service. No separate Identra user store to keep in sync.

Drop-in PHP module

A thin PHP integration layer sits in front of your app's login page. Most teams are live in under an hour.

Zero new infrastructure

No extra database, no extra service to patch. Identra talks to what you already run.
